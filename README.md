🚨 GuardianSOS – Personal Emergency Alert App

GuardianSOS is an Android personal safety application designed to act as your trusted emergency companion. In dangerous or urgent situations, a single tap can alert your trusted contacts, share your location, activate a loud siren, and record critical evidence — providing peace of mind for you and your loved ones.

📱 Features
🔴 One-Tap SOS

Trigger a full emergency response instantly with a single press of the SOS button.

📞 Primary Emergency Call

Automatically places a phone call to your designated primary emergency contact.

📩 Multi-Contact SMS Alerts

Sends an emergency SMS to multiple trusted contacts

Includes your real-time GPS location

Fully customizable message

🔊 Loud Siren

Activates a high-volume siren to:

Attract nearby attention

Deter potential threats

🎥 Evidence Recording

Capture crucial evidence during emergencies:

Video Recording
Quickly launches the device camera to record video.

Audio Recording
Discreet in-app voice recorder with a timer.

🔗 Flexible Evidence Sharing

Instantly share recorded audio or video using any compatible app:

WhatsApp

Telegram

Google Drive

Email, and more

👥 Trusted Contacts Management

Add contacts manually or sync from your phone’s address book

Assign:

One primary contact for emergency calls

Multiple contacts for SMS alerts

Simple toggle & checkbox controls

🏠 Home Screen Widget

Trigger an SOS directly from your home screen — no need to open the app.

🔐 Permissions Used

GuardianSOS requests only essential permissions to deliver its safety features:

Permission	Purpose
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION	Share your GPS location in emergency messages
CALL_PHONE	Automatically call your primary emergency contact
SEND_SMS	Send emergency SMS alerts
READ_CONTACTS	Sync trusted contacts from your address book
RECORD_AUDIO	Enable audio evidence recording
CAMERA	Record video evidence
FOREGROUND_SERVICE	Keep the siren running in the background
🛠️ How to Use
1️⃣ Set Up Trusted Contacts

Open the app → Trusted Contacts

Add contacts:

Manually

From address book

By syncing

Select:

One contact as Primary Call

Others for SMS alerts

2️⃣ Customize Emergency Message

Go to Settings

Edit the default emergency message sent via SMS

3️⃣ Activate SOS

Press the SOS button

The app will:

Call the primary contact

Send SMS alerts with your location

4️⃣ Use Quick Actions

SIREN – Activate loud alarm

RECORD VIDEO – Capture video evidence

RECORD VOICE – Record audio discreetly

SEND MESSAGE – Send emergency SMS without calling

GuardianSOS is a supportive safety tool and should not replace official emergency services. Always contact local emergency authorities when possible.
